## What HRT judges will look for (design every feature around this)
HRT is a quant trading firm. A pretty chart is not enough. Our project must show:
1. **Real data → a prediction**, evaluated honestly on held-out data. Show the actual accuracy / error, including where it fails.
2. **Probabilities, not yes/no.** Calibrated where possible ("72% likely"), with uncertainty shown in the UI.
3. **A decision made from the prediction** — "therefore do X", with the expected value or trade-off spelled out. This is the step most teams skip.
4. A baseline to beat (naive / last-value / majority class), so the model's lift is visible.
Never fake data or accuracy numbers; judges will ask how they were measured.

## What we are building — LOCKED (13:15 Sat)
**Working name: "Horizon" — is the prediction-market time bias actually tradable?**

Published research (arXiv 2602.19520, 292M trades on Kalshi + Polymarket) shows prediction-market prices are miscalibrated *as a function of time to resolution*: far from resolution, prices are compressed toward 50% (a 70¢ contract a month out resolves YES more like ~75% of the time), with domain differences (politics underconfident, etc.). That paper measures calibration. **We ask the trader's question: does that edge survive spreads, fees and an honest out-of-time test — and when (how far from resolution) is it worth acting?**

Pipeline:
1. **Data (Role C):** resolved *binary* Polymarket markets → market price at fixed horizons before resolution (30d, 7d, 3d, 1d, 12h) + outcome + domain + volume/liquidity. Manifold as backup / extra sample.
2. **Model (Role A):** calibration map q = f(price, horizon, domain) — logistic regression on logit(price) with horizon × domain terms. **Baseline = the market price itself.** Metrics: Brier, log loss, reliability curves, on an **out-of-time** test set (train on markets resolved before a cutoff date, test after).
3. **Decision (Role A):** edge = q − price; trade only if edge > costs (spread + fee); size by **fractional Kelly (¼)**. Backtest P&L on the test set **with and without costs**: return, hit rate, max drawdown. Compare to naive "always back the favourite".
4. **Live (Roles A + C):** currently open markets → q, edge, recommended action ("buy YES / buy NO / **no trade — costs eat the edge**"), Kelly size, and *when* the edge is expected to be largest.
5. **Screen (Role B):** live decision cards + calibration chart by horizon + backtest equity curve with/without costs + an honest "where it fails" panel.

Pitch line (**FINAL, Sun 00:30: policy COH**): *"Published research says prediction-market prices are miscalibrated depending on time to resolution. On 34,677 Polymarket markets, tested out of time on 10,579, we find prices get overconfident as resolution nears — 10–35¢ longshots win more often than priced, favourites less; a month out they are about fair. A 4-coefficient model that corrects this beats the market, and the rule that trades it — only within 24 hours of the end, at least 1¢ of net edge after fees, probabilities scaled to sum to 100% across an event's outcomes and one bet per event — earned +31¢ per $1 staked after Polymarket's fees (90% CI +22¢ to +41¢) on 1,827 bets, against +17¢ for the same model without the event rule, with half the drawdown; total profit about the same. Ten variants were compared on validation; the test was opened once for the two finalists. Mostly sports; culture is the one domain that lost."* Say plainly: **the cited paper found prices compressed toward 50% far from resolution; we did NOT reproduce that direction — on our test prices are about fair a month out and too extreme near resolution.** Cite the paper; never claim we discovered the bias.

Honesty rules (quant judges will probe these): binary markets only; drop cancelled / MKT resolutions; split by **time**, never random; include costs; report where there is **no** edge as clearly as where there is.

Stretch (only after the 16:30 gate works): Gemini classifies Polymarket's unlabelled "Other" markets into domains (the paper notes ~42% are unclassified) — real use of the Gemini prize.

### Results (Role A, FINAL Sun 00:30: policy COH; V3 kept beside it) — team dataset (Ayaz, 34,677 markets), numbers in `backend/results/*.json` (`headline`, `policy_comparison`), `integrations/ensemble/results.json`, `integrations/robustness/`, model `2026-10-24-390c312`

V3 +17.2¢, 3,653 trades, maxDD 0.38. Brier 0.18208 → 0.18180. - **Money (V3, the previous headline, kept as the comparison):** trade only if net edge after fees + model uncertainty ≥ 1¢ and ≤ 24 h before the scheduled end (earlier = WAIT); ¼ Kelly capped 5%; open stake ≤ 1 starting bankroll (pro rata at ties); test trades must be entered on/after the cutoff (embargo). **+17.2¢ per $1 staked after fees, 90% CI [+11.8¢, +23.5¢]; +4.01 starting bankrolls, CI [2.78, 5.44]; 3,653 trades; hit rate 22%; max drawdown 0.38; peak open stake 0.69.** Rule chosen on validation among V0–V3 (`integrations/playoff`, validation per $1: V0 +22.0¢, V2 +24.3¢, V3 +25.5¢ — differences inside the CIs); test results for all four side by side (V1 and V2 were proposed by teammates who had seen test numbers, so the choice used validation only): V0 +11.1¢ (+1.76 bankrolls), V2 +16.3¢, V3 +17.2¢. Naive "always back the favourite": −8.70. Older headlines (+3.05, +190%, +12.7¢) are retired.
- **Method / limitations (state them):** entry price = the last print at or before the target time in Polymarket's hourly price history (prior prints only, ≤6 h old; 98.9% of V3 trades ≤1 h old); costs = max(current quoted half-spread, 1¢) + Polymarket taker fee — historical spreads are not available, a stated limitation. **Robustness (V3, `robustness` in backtest.json):** prices ≤1 h old +17.3¢ [11.3, 23.5] (3,611 trades); markets that closed ≥12 h after the price +16.9¢ [11.1, 22.8]; both +16.9¢ [11.3, 22.9], +3.82 bankrolls (3,549 trades).

Volume $100k–1M +16.5¢, >$1M +21.3¢ (not only thin markets). Crypto priced as options from Binance (C, 1,106 markets): Brier 0.1471 vs market 0.1399, −16.5¢ per $1. Never say "sports is well calibrated".
- **Robustness (TheMarkz3, `integrations/robustness`):** V3 edge positive in 13 of 15 months (CI below 0 in none), all 5 test months positive; placebo "prices are right" (outcome ~ Bernoulli(price)) on the same trades −9.8¢ [−15.9, −3.5] vs real +17.2¢, p = 0/1000; deflated Sharpe 0.45 among 15 correlated trials, 0.9999 among the 4 playoff trials, test PSR 0.999996; survives double Polymarket fees (+15.7¢); capacity ~$10k per market on liquid football books, ~$1k on typical ones (one live snapshot). Polymarket gotcha: for closed markets `prices-history` with `interval=max` can come back empty or 12h-coarse — request explicit `startTs`/`endTs` in ~15-day chunks. 12h granularity is fine for our horizons.
**Horizon anchor (Role A, 13:50):** measure `horizon_hours` back from the market's **scheduled `endDate`**, not `closedTime` — live markets only know `endDate`, and markets that close early (e.g. a team knocked out) would leak the outcome.
