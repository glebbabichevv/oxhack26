## Demo script (≈3 min, three moments)
1. **Insight (30 s), site hero.** "Polymarket prices are about fair a month out and get overconfident as the end nears: a day out, 15¢ contracts win 20%." One number: "+17.2¢ per $1 staked after fees on 10,579 markets the model never saw."
2. **Decision where people trade (60 s), Polymarket match page + Horizon panel.** Market > 24h: "Wait — best time tonight 21:00–08:00 (why: bets within 24h earned ~14–17¢ per $1, a week out ~2¢)". Market < 24h: "Market 16% · Horizon 20% · Price too low" → Set up $X → the ticket fills itself → the human presses Trade (we don't).
3. **Agent + proof (60 s).** "Set up my 3 best bets tonight" → plan + tickets filled → open /journal: every decision with outcome, cumulative per-$1 line, and the capsule sealed at 16:31 Saturday (hash, OpenTimestamps, Discord) opened at 12:30 — "whatever it says".
Close (10 s): "Not just what to bet — when. And we show where it doesn't work."

## Slides (3–4)

## Quant Q&A crib (one line each)
- Entry price: last trade ≤ 1h before the target time, hourly history, prior-only; costs = max(current half-spread, 1¢) + Polymarket taker fee; historical spreads unavailable (limitation).
- Stale prices / event already over: re-run on V3 → see item 1 numbers.
- Selection bias: rule chosen on validation (last 20% of train); test shown for all variants.
- Exposure: ≤ 1 bankroll open, pro-rata for simultaneous entries; bankroll result only with the "mostly sports" caveat.
- Paper says compression toward 50% far out; we did not reproduce that direction.
