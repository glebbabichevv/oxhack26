{\LARGE\bfseries Horizon} \hfill {\small OxHack '26 \textperiodcentered{} \url{https://github.com/glebbabichevv/oxhack26}}\\[2pt]
{\small Polymarket prices are miscalibrated as a function of \emph{time to resolution}. Does correcting that survive spreads, fees and an honest out-of-time test, and \emph{when} is it worth acting?}

\begin{multicols}{2}

\sect{1. Data}
\begin{itemize}
\item 34{,}677 resolved \textbf{binary} Polymarket markets (Gamma + CLOB hourly price history). Cancelled / 50--50 resolutions dropped.
\item One row per (market, horizon) with $h \in \{720, 168, 72, 24, 12\}$ hours before the \textbf{scheduled} \texttt{endDate} (live markets only know that; \texttt{closedTime} would leak early closes).
\item Entry price = last trade print at or before the target time, prior prints only, $\le 6$\,h old (98.9\% $\le 1$\,h). Prices kept in $[3\%, 97\%]$.
\item Domain from event tags: sports, politics, crypto, culture, economics, science/tech, geopolitics, other. Lifetime volume $\ge \$100$k.
\end{itemize}

\sect{2. Out-of-time split}
\begin{itemize}
\item Train: events that ended before \textbf{2026-05-18} (23{,}838 markets). Test: the 10{,}579 after.
\item Legs of one event never straddle the split; 260 legs that resolved before the cutoff dropped from test.
\item Embargo: a test trade counts only if entered on/after the cutoff.
\item Validation = last 20\% of train events (model and rule selection happen here only).
\end{itemize}

\sect{3. The model (M0): 4 coefficients}
With price $p$ and hours $h$, let $\ell = \logit p = \ln\frac{p}{1-p}$ and $\tau = \ln\frac{\mathrm{clip}(h,12,720)}{24}$:
\[
q \;=\; \sigma\!\left(\beta_0 + \beta_1\,\ell + \beta_2\,\tau + \beta_3\,\ell\,\tau\right),\qquad \sigma(x)=\frac{1}{1+e^{-x}}
\]
Fitted (logistic regression, train only):
$\beta_0 = 0.066,\ \beta_1 = 0.845,\ \beta_2 = -0.015,\ \beta_3 = 0.022$.\\
Slope $\beta_1 + \beta_3\tau < 1$ near the end $\Rightarrow$ prices are \textbf{overconfident} there: longshots win more than priced, favourites less; a month out ($\tau \approx 3.4$) the slope is $\approx 0.92$, about fair.

\textbf{Uncertainty band.} 40 refits on bootstrap resamples \emph{by event}; $[q_{10}, q_{90}]$ = 10th/90th percentile of their predictions.

\sect{4. Costs}
\[
c = \max\!\left(\tfrac{\text{ask}-\text{bid}}{2},\ 1\text{\textcent}\right) + r\,\bigl(p(1-p)\bigr)^{e}
\]
Polymarket taker fee with each market's own \texttt{feeSchedule} (rate $r$, exponent $e$; default rates 4--7\% by domain). Historical spreads are unavailable, so the 1\textcent{} floor is a stated limitation.

\sect{5. Decision rule}
Net edge on the conservative end of the band:
\[
e_{\text{YES}} = q_{10} - (p + c), \qquad e_{\text{NO}} = (p - c) - q_{90}
\]
Back YES if $e_{\text{YES}} > 0$, NO if $e_{\text{NO}} > 0$, else no trade. \textbf{Policy V3} adds: net edge $\ge 1$\textcent, and only within \textbf{24\,h} of the end (earlier $\Rightarrow$ WAIT). No call under 12\,h (never trained or tested there).

\textbf{Stake: quarter Kelly, capped.} For YES at cost $\pi = p + c$:
\[
f = \min\!\left(\tfrac14\cdot\frac{q_{10} - \pi}{1 - \pi},\ 5\%\right)
\]
Open stake $\le 1$ starting bankroll (later entries get what is left, pro rata at ties). P\&L additive, no compounding.

\sect{6. Policy COH (shipped)}
In a neg-risk event whose legs at one horizon number $\ge 2$ and whose prices sum to $[0.95, 1.25]$:
\[
q_i \leftarrow \frac{q_i}{\sum_j q_j},\qquad \text{band offsets } q_i - q_{10,i},\ q_{90,i} - q_i \text{ kept}
\]
then V3, then \textbf{one bet per event} (largest net edge; ties broken by market id, stable sort). Thin legs ($<\$100$k) price the book but get no call.

\sect{7. Metrics}
\[
\text{Brier} = \tfrac1n\textstyle\sum (q-y)^2
\]
\[
\text{LogLoss} = -\tfrac1n\textstyle\sum \bigl[y\ln q + (1-y)\ln(1-q)\bigr]
\]
Return per \$1 staked $= \sum_k f_k r_k \big/ \sum_k f_k$, with payoff $r = \frac{y}{p+c} - 1$ (YES) or $\frac{1-y}{1-p+c} - 1$ (NO).
90\% intervals: bootstrap resampling \emph{events} (legs of a match move together), 1{,}000--2{,}000 draws, seed 0.

\sect{10. Robustness}
\begin{itemize}
\item Stale prices: only prints $\le 1$\,h old and markets closed $\ge 12$\,h after the print: COH +29.6\textcent{} [21.1, 39.7].
\item Placebo ``prices are right'' ($y \sim \text{Bernoulli}(p)$, 1{,}000 draws, same trades): $-8.1$\textcent{} median vs real +31\textcent, $p < 0.001$.
\item Walk-forward by month (refit on earlier events): COH positive in 15 of 17 months.
\item Deflated Sharpe 0.9999 over 12 trials (per-trade SR 0.135).
\item Survives double Polymarket fees (+15.7\textcent{} V3). Capacity $\approx\$10$k per market on liquid football books.
\item The gain is one bet per event, not leg picking: a random eligible leg gives +33.5\textcent.
\end{itemize}

\sect{11. Sealed predictions}
Live predictions frozen to a file; its SHA-256 posted in the event Discord before the markets closed and anchored in Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps (Seal 1 block 972944, Seal 2 block 972999). Pre-registered metric: Brier vs the market with an event-bootstrap 90\% interval; only markets resolved after the post count.
